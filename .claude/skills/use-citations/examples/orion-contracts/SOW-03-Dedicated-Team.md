# Statement of Work No. 3 - Dedicated Analytics Team

**Reference:** Master Services Agreement dated March 1, 2024 between Northwind Analytics, Inc.
and Orion Freight Holdings, LLC.
**SOW Effective Date:** April 1, 2025
**SOW Term:** Eighteen (18) months from the SOW Effective Date.

## 1. Scope

Provider will staff a dedicated team of six (6) full-time analytics engineers supporting
Customer's freight-routing platform, including data pipeline development, model
maintenance, and on-call support during Customer's business hours.

## 2. Fees and Minimum Commitment

Customer commits to a minimum of $2,160,000 over the SOW Term, invoiced as $120,000 per
month. Customer shall prepay the first two (2) months upon execution of this SOW.

Because Provider has hired dedicated personnel for this engagement, Customer acknowledges
that the minimum commitment is not subject to reduction for unused capacity.

## 3. Termination

This SOW expressly supersedes Section 11.1 of the Agreement with respect to this SOW
only. Customer may terminate this SOW for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior
written notice, provided that Customer pays an early termination fee equal to fifty
percent (50%) of the remaining minimum commitment as of the effective date of
termination.

Provider may not terminate this SOW for convenience during the SOW Term.

## 4. Acceptance

Deliverables are deemed accepted ten (10) business days after delivery unless Customer
provides written notice of non-conformance describing the deficiency in reasonable detail.
