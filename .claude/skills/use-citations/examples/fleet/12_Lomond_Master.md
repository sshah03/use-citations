MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as of March 13, 2021 between Orion Freight Holdings, LLC ("Buyer") and Lomond Textiles, Inc. ("Supplier").

1. SCOPE

Supplier shall provide the goods and services described in the schedules to this Agreement. Buyer shall issue purchase orders referencing this Agreement; no preprinted terms on a purchase order have any effect.

2. TERM AND RENEWAL

The initial term is thirty-six (36) months and expires at the end of that term unless the parties sign a written extension. There is no automatic renewal.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

Buyer shall pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of receipt. Supplier may not increase prices more than once in any twelve (12) month period, and then only on ninety (90) days' prior written notice.

4. TERMINATION

Termination for Convenience. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon one hundred twenty (120) days' prior written notice to the other party.

Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other party commits a material breach that remains uncured for thirty (30) days after notice.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party shall protect the other's Confidential Information with the same degree of care it uses for its own, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care. These obligations survive for five (5) years after termination.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Neither party is liable for lost profits or for indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages. The parties have not agreed any aggregate cap on direct damages under this Agreement.

7. INDEMNIFICATION

Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Buyer against third-party claims alleging that the goods infringe any patent, copyright or trade secret.

8. ASSIGNMENT

Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld. A change of control of either party is deemed an assignment requiring consent under this Section.

9. INSURANCE

Supplier shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and shall name Buyer as an additional insured.

10. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Washington, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Washington.

11. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is the entire agreement of the parties and supersedes all prior proposals. No amendment is effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.
